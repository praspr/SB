# Signals

## Allowed tools
- `search_signals`

## Procedure
1. Search with terms narrow enough to identify relevant local evidence.
2. Distinguish observed evidence from inference.
3. Cite every used result as `[signal:N]` with its returned signal id.
4. Never invent a signal or cite a result that was not returned.
